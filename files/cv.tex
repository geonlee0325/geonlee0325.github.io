\section*{Research Summary}
\vspace{5pt}
My research explores how interactions among relational data, retrieved knowledge, and AI agents shape learning, reasoning, and behavior. By uncovering their underlying principles, I aim to develop intelligent systems that effectively leverage diverse information while remaining reliable, generalizable, and robust in real-world settings.
